Agreement

At Slough Tuition Centre (STC), we promise to:

  • Provide a safe, supportive, and engaging environment where your child can truly thrive.
  • Support your child in reaching their full potential as a learner and a valued member of the STC community.
  • Inspire students to take responsibility for their learning and celebrate their achievements.
  • Keep you regularly informed about your child's progress and updates on STC activities and initiatives.
  • Enforce STC's behaviour and anti-bullying policies to ensure a respectful and inclusive atmosphere for all.
  • Provide homework for all students based on what best supports the child's learning.
  • Reserve the right to cancel any session due to unforeseen circumstances.
